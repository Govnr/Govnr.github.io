---
title: Rebuilding Govnr
lede: Govnr is being revived as open civic infrastructure for direct democracy.
---

Govnr began as an early experiment in online direct democracy. The project is now being rebuilt around modern architecture, a clearer civic domain model, verifiable voting records, and accessible participation.

The first work is focused on specification: organisations, spaces, membership, petitions, motions, voting, results, decisions, auditability, and AI assistance that supports people without replacing democratic judgment.
